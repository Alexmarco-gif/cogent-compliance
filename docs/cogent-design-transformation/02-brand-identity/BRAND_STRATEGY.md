**Category:** Nigerian Financial Regulatory Intelligence & Supervisory Compliance Operating System  
**Primary Market:** CBN-, FCCPC-, ARCON-, NDPC-, and SEC-regulated Nigerian financial institutions  
**Brand Archetype:** The Sovereign Architect & Verifier (authoritative precision, institutional trust, operational velocity)

---

## 1. Brand Positioning Statement

For Nigerian financial institutions, digital lenders, microfinance banks, payment service banks, and investment platforms whose product launches are slowed by multi-day manual compliance reviews—or exposed to ₦100M+ statutory penalties and app-store delistings—**Cogent** is the **Institutional Compliance Operating System** that verifies every promotional claim, rate calculation, flyer layout, and live URL against Nigerian statutory law and internal rate cards in seconds, producing a tamper-evident audit dossier before anything goes live.

Unlike generic AI chatbots that hallucinate citations or external retainers that take 5–10 days, Cogent pairs a **verified Nigerian statutory corpus** with **deterministic financial & mechanical locks** and **role-based CCO sign-off**.

## 3. Verbal Identity & Voice Rules

### Voice Characteristics
- **Direct & Institutional:** Short, declarative sentences grounded in operational reality.
- **Legally Literate without Legalese Bloat:** Cite exact instruments (`CBN CPF §4.2`, `FCCPA §115`, `ARCON ASP`, `NDPA 2023`, `BOFIA 2020`) naturally as proof, not academic jargon.
- **Consequence-Aware, Never Fear-Mongering:** State concrete regulatory exposures (`₦1M–₦3M` ARCON penalties, `₦100M / 1% turnover` FCCPC fines, `<60-day` Play Store removal) factually.

### Copywriting Do's & Don'ts
- **DO write:** *"Verify every claim, rate, and flyer before it goes live."*
- **DO write:** *"Deterministic APR math meets verified CBN, FCCPC, and ARCON law."*
- **DO NOT write:** *"Supercharge your compliance with magical AI!"*
- **DO NOT write:** *"100% legal guarantee—fire your lawyers!"* (Violates Product Non-Goals in `Document 01 §2.3`)
- **DO NOT use:** Gaming slang, exclamation-mark hype, or emoji bullets (`🚀`, `✨`, `💡`).

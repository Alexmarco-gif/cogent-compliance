# Creative Direction: "Sovereign Regulatory Authority"

**Mandate:** Match the strategic discipline, editorial precision, and product-led clarity of Goodface's PayOp redesign—while forging an original, unmistakable institutional identity for Nigerian regulatory intelligence.

---

## 1. Strategic Creative Thesis

> **Cogent is not an AI writing assistant. It is Nigeria's Institutional Compliance Operating System.**

Where PayOp needed to break free from stuffy legacy banking templates to win digital-first gaming merchants, **Cogent must break free from generic AI-SaaS templates** (purple gradients, glowing chat bubbles, robot vectors, floating glass cards) to earn the immediate trust of Chief Compliance Officers, General Counsel, and CBN-regulated bank executives.

Our creative direction is **"Sovereign Regulatory Authority"**:
- **Architectural, not decorative:** Every border, grid line, and card edge is `1px` crisp (`#E4E4E7`), deliberate, and structurally aligned—reminiscent of a legal ledger, central bank circular, and Linear/Stripe engineering precision.
- **Product-led, not abstract:** Instead of vague metaphors, the marketing website *shows Cogent thinking*: real statutory citations (`CBN CPF §4.2`, `FCCPA §115`, `ARCON Code §4.2`), real APR drip-pricing comparisons, real flyer bounding-box checks, and real SHA-256 dossier seals.
- **Restrained contrast:** A predominantly bright alabaster and crisp white editorial canvas (`90%+` light surface discipline matching the authenticated workspace in Document 12) punctuated by **Deep Obsidian (`#09090B`)** authority bands and **Sovereign Cobalt (`#2A4BFF`)** precision signals (matching `AuthSplitLayout.tsx` and `CogentLogo variant="blue"`).

---

## 2. Six Core Creative Principles (Translated from Goodface Methodology)

1. **Distinctive Institutional Credibility:** Lead with calm, sharp editorial typography (`Instrument Serif` + `Inter` + `JetBrains Mono`) and verifiable Nigerian statutory references—never hype.
2. **Visual Regulatory Storytelling:** Communicate complex mechanisms (3-Source Grounding, Drip-Pricing APR Math, License-Scope Boundaries, URL Drift Diffs) through interactive, inspectable visual modules rather than walls of marketing prose.
3. **Product-Led Proof:** Every major capability claim is paired directly with an authentic, interactive Cogent UI vignette (Pre-Flight Gate banner, Flyer Forensics bounding box, KFS table, Dossier seal).
4. **Architectural Information Hierarchy:** Asymmetric bento grids, crisp 2-column feature-to-preview splits, and segmented license switchers prevent monotonous repeated card grids.
5. **End-to-End Surface Continuity:** A visitor moving from `/` (Landing) -> `/solutions/*` -> `/pricing` -> `/sign-up` -> `/onboarding` -> `/chat` experiences **one unified brand**—the exact same logo mark, typography scale, hairline border system, cobalt/obsidian accent grammar, and semantic colors.
6. **Purposeful, Measured Motion:** Motion explains state transitions (switching license scopes, running a simulated pre-flight lock check, sliding open an evidence citation drawer, revealing a URL drift redline)—never bouncing stickers or gratuitous parallax.

## 3. Visual Personality Matrix

| Attribute | We Are | We Are Never |
|---|---|---|
| **Tone** | Authoritative, precise, verifiable, calm | Playful, sarcastic (`"Suck"`), hype-driven, casual |
| **Color Discipline** | Crisp Alabaster/White (`#FFFFFF`/`#FAFAFA`), Obsidian (`#09090B`), Sovereign Cobalt (`#2A4BFF`), Semantic Crimson/Emerald/Amber | Neon purple-pink AI gradients, rainbow pastel pills, dark cyberpunk glow |
| **Typography** | Editorial Serif display headlines + crisp Inter UI + tabular JetBrains Mono for citations & Naira figures | Heavy rounded comic fonts, tilted sticker badges, inconsistent sizes |
| **Visuals** | Framed interactive Cogent workspace views, precision hairline diagrams, structured KFS/dossier sheets | Generic AI robots, floating 3D game icons, stock photos of handshakes |
| **Layout** | Intentional negative space, asymmetric bento compositions, strict 12-column grid alignment | Cluttered wall-to-wall gradients, identical 3×2 icon-card grids repeated down the page |

## 4. Signature Visual Motifs Unique to Cogent

1. **The Verification Ledger Frame:** Product demonstrations sit inside crisp `1px` bordered architectural frames with a subtle status rail (`LOCK 1–3 • DETERMINISTIC`, `SHA-256 VERIFIED`, `CBN / FCCPC / ARCON`), visually reinforcing auditability.
2. **Inline Statutory Provenance Chips:** Just like Cogent's live chat stream, marketing explanations use crisp monospace citation tags (`[CBN CPF §3.2]`, `[FCCPC DEON Reg. 18]`) that visitors can hover or click to inspect the real obligation.
3. **Before/After Compliance Diff:** Showing raw risky copy (`"0% hidden charges, instant guaranteed loan"`) transformed with emerald-highlighted statutory qualifiers (`"Effective APR: 64.8% p.a. • Subject to KYC & credit assessment • Licensed by CBN"`).
4. **The Cobalt & Alabaster Atmospheric Anchor:** Adapting the subtle diagonal cobalt-to-alabaster architectural surface already proven in `src/components/auth/AuthSplitLayout.tsx` for select executive hero/CTA showcase panels—creating instant visual synergy between marketing and auth.

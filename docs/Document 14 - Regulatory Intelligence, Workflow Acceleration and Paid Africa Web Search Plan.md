# 2. Root-Cause Operational Pain: Why Nigerian Fintech Compliance Takes 3–10 Days

In a typical 30–350 person Nigerian fintech or MFB, a single product launch, policy update, or partner bank audit takes **3 to 10 business days** (and cross-border expansion scoping takes **2 to 3 weeks** and **$15,000+ USD** in outside counsel retainers) for five structural reasons:

1. **Extreme Agency Fragmentation (7+ Overlapping Authorities):** A single modern fintech feature (e.g., a *USD virtual dollar card funded from a Naira wallet with BNPL installments*) simultaneously touches **CBN** (FX, Payment Services, KYC Tiers, Consumer Protection Framework), **NDPC** (NDPA 2023 cross-border data transfer rules & GAID 2025), **FCCPC** (digital credit & fair pricing), **NFIU** (AML/CFT/CPF transaction reporting), **NDIC** (pass-through deposit insurance eligibility), **ARCON** (pre-vetted launch communications), and **FIRS** (EMT levy / VAT on fees).
2. **Dark, Unindexed `.gov.ng` PDF Repositories:** Official portals (`cbn.gov.ng`, `sec.gov.ng`, `ndpc.gov.ng`, `fccpc.gov.ng`, `nfiu.gov.ng`) publish critical rules inside unindexed, inconsistently titled PDFs buried across nested directory paths (`BSD/DIR/GEN/LAB/...`, `PSM/DIR/CON/...`, exposure drafts, corrigenda). Standard LLM web search frequently misses them or confuses superseded 2019 drafts with 2025/2026 amendments.
3. **Zero Link Between External Rules and Internal Documents:** When CBN drops a new circular, an LLM cannot read the institution's uploaded 80-page AML/CFT Manual, Agency Banking SOP, or active product schedule (`campaign_products`) alongside that circular to identify *which exact internal paragraphs are now non-compliant*.
4. **Partner Bank, NIBSS & Regulator DDQ Bottlenecks:** Sponsor banks (`Providus`, `Wema`, `Sterling`, `Parallex`), NIBSS, card schemes, and regulators require multi-question compliance questionnaires (`20–60` questions) backed by both statutory citations and internal policy references—forcing CCOs to spend days hunting paragraphs across Confluence, Google Drive, and old emails.
5. **No Verifiable Work Product Canvas:** Even when teams use generic AI chat, they receive unstructured text walls containing fabricated sections or generic disclaimers—forcing Legal Counsel to rewrite memos, matrices, and Word documents completely from scratch.

## 3.2 Detailed Behavioral Flows for Workflows 1–4

### Workflow 1: Product & Feature Structuring Research (`PRODUCT_STRUCTURING_MEMO`)
1. **Trigger:** Prompt describing a planned product, feature change, fee model, FX rail, wallet tier, or partner integration (e.g., *"We hold an MFB Tier-1 license and want to launch an interest-bearing USD savings pocket plus instant Naira BNPL. What CBN, NDIC, NDPC, SEC, and FCCPC rules apply?"*).
2. **Pre-Hydration & Reasoning:**
   - Loads tenant's active `regulatory_scopes`, `institution_license_tier`, and existing `campaign_products` (`precedentMemoryService`).
   - Runs **Track A** (141-PDF Statutory Vault + tenant playbooks), **Track B** (SerpAPI `.gov.ng` circular/PDF search), and **Track C** (Exa AI clean text extraction across official portals + Tier-1 Nigerian legal alerts).
   - Evaluates license-scope constraints deterministically via `licenseScopeService` (e.g., flagging whether an MFB vs. DMB vs. PSB vs. FCCPC Digital Lender can legally custody FX or offer credit).
3. **Structured Canvas Deliverable:** Populates `WorkProductCanvas` with a formal **Statutory Structuring Memo** containing:
   - *Permissibility & License Verdict* (`PERMISSIBLE`, `PERMISSIBLE_WITH_PARTNER_BANK_SPONSORSHIP`, `ADDITIONAL_LICENSE_REQUIRED`, or `PROHIBITED`)
   - *Multi-Agency Breakdown Table* (Regulator | Applicable Circular / Statute | Statutory Thresholds & Operational Mandates | Source Trust Badge)
   - *Mandatory Product Disclosures & Customer T&C Requirements*
   - *Pre-Launch Legal & Regulatory Action Checklist*

### Workflow 2: Internal Policy & SOP Drafting / Gap Redlining (`POLICY_GAP_REDLINE`)
1. **Trigger:** User attaches a policy PDF/DOCX or references an internal playbook uploaded in `/knowledge-base` (`marketing_knowledge_documents`), paired with a target circular/law (e.g., *"Compare our AML/CFT & Onboarding Policy against the newest 2025/2026 CBN Automated AML Baseline and Agent Banking circulars, and draft exact clause updates."*).
2. **Pre-Hydration & Reasoning:**
   - Loads structured sections/chunks from `marketing_knowledge_chunks` or `asset_extraction_segments` of the internal policy (never dropping page/section identifiers).
   - Retrieves target regulations from **Track A (Vault)** and **Track B + C (Live SerpAPI + Exa AI)** for recent amendments/exposure drafts.
   - Identifies **4 Gap Classifications**: `CONTRADICTS_NEW_MANDATE`, `OMITS_NEW_OBLIGATION`, `OUTDATED_THRESHOLD_OR_SLA`, `TERMINOLOGY_DRIFT`.
   - **Legal Floor Rule:** Proposed redlines may strengthen internal controls or match statutory baselines verbatim, but guardrails block any proposed clause that weakens mandatory CBN/NFIU/NDPC thresholds (e.g., shortening record retention below 5 years under MLPPA/BOFIA or relaxing KYC Tier limits).
3. **Structured Canvas Deliverable:** Populates `WorkProductCanvas` with:
   - *Executive Policy Gap Scorecard* (`Critical Gaps`, `Moderate Updates`, `Aligned Sections`)
   - *Side-by-Side Redline Matrix*: Internal Section Reference | Current Policy Text | New Statutory Mandate (`[Citation]`) | Gap Analysis | **Ready-to-Insert Compliant Replacement Clause**
   - *Clean Consolidated Clauses Tab* ready to copy or export directly into `.docx` for Board Risk Committee ratification.

### Workflow 3: Regulator Inquiry, Audit & Partner Due-Diligence Responses (`SUPERVISORY_DDQ_RESPONSE`)
1. **Trigger:** User uploads a regulator examination letter, NIBSS onboarding checklist, or sponsor-bank DDQ (`PDF`/`DOCX`/table), or pastes numbered due-diligence questions.
2. **Pre-Hydration & Reasoning:**
   - Deterministic parser extracts discrete numbered questions (`Q1..Qn`, max 50 items per turn or durable Inngest batch for large uploads).
   - For each question, Cogent executes hybrid retrieval across **both** the institution's private **Knowledge Base & Rate Cards** (`marketing_knowledge_chunks`, `marketing_knowledge_rules`, `campaign_products`, `tenants`) and **Verified Law** (`regulatory_clauses` + gated Live Web).
   - **Strict Truthfulness & Anti-Fabrication Mandate (`Internal Evidence Firewall`):**
     - **Law** comes from Vault + Live Official Circulars.
     - **Institutional Operational Fact** (e.g., *"We use X vendor for PEP screening"* or *"Our Board Audit Committee meets quarterly"*) may **ONLY** be asserted if explicitly verified in the tenant's uploaded playbooks, SOPs, product configs, or user message.
     - Whenever internal operational proof is absent for a question, Cogent drafts the statutory framework cleanly and inserts an explicit structured callout: `[INTERNAL_VERIFICATION_REQUIRED: Confirm internal control owner / screening cadence / artifact evidence before submission]`.
3. **Structured Canvas Deliverable:** Populates `WorkProductCanvas` with:
   - *Formal Cover Letter / Supervisory Transmittal Template* (institution letterhead placeholders, reference numbers)
   - *Question-by-Question Response Schedule* (`Q# | Question | Drafted Citation-Backed Answer | Internal Policy Evidence | Statutory Basis | Completeness Badge: GROUNDED / NEEDS_INTERNAL_EVIDENCE`)
   - Filter toggle to view `"Needs Verification Only"` so the CCO clears gaps in minutes before `.docx` export.

### Workflow 4: Cross-Border & Pan-African Expansion Research (`CROSS_BORDER_COMPARISON`)
1. **Trigger:** User selects Pan-African jurisdiction toggle (available on **Scale ($449/mo)** and **Enterprise ($1,499/mo)**) or asks a cross-border comparison question (e.g., *"Compare Nigeria CBN PSSP licensing, capital requirements, settlement timelines, and NDPA localization rules with Kenya CBK PSP and Ghana BoG Enhanced PSP."*).
2. **Pre-Hydration & Reasoning:**
   - Track A provides the verified **Nigeria Baseline** from the 141-PDF Statutory Vault.
   - Track B (`SerpAPI` country-scoped `gl=ng|ke|gh|za|rw|eg`, `site:.go.ke`, `site:.gov.gh`, `site:.co.za`, `site:.rw`) finds primary central bank/regulator PDFs and gazetted licensing schedules.
   - Track C (`Exa AI`) extracts clean statutory tables and comparative analysis from Tier-1 African commercial law firms (`Bowmans`, `ENSafrica`, `Anjarwalla & Khanna / ALN`, `Bentsi-Enchill`, `Banwo & Ighodalo`, `Templars`, `Udo Udoma`).
   - Synthesizes normalized attributes with explicit retrieval dates and trust tiers per cell.
3. **Structured Canvas Deliverable:** Populates `WorkProductCanvas` with:
   - *Normalized Cross-Jurisdictional Matrix* (`Regulatory Dimension` rows × `Country` columns: Licensing Authority, License Tier, Minimum Capital, Local Entity/Ownership & Governance Requirements, Passporting / FX Restrictions, Data Protection & Cross-Border Transfer Authority, Consumer & Marketing Pre-Clearance, Estimated Licensing Window)
   - *Divergence & Operational Design Alerts* (e.g., highlighting Kenya ODPC registration vs. Nigeria NDPC MIRO/DPO thresholds, or Ghana BoG local equity requirements)
   - *Source Freshness Ledger* linking every cell to timestamped official/legal briefings and advising formal local counsel validation prior to filing.

# 4. Dual-Engine Nigeria & Africa Live Web Search Architecture (`Exa AI` + `SerpAPI` + `141-PDF Vault`)

## 4.1 Why Single-Engine LLM Search Fails on African Financial Regulation
Previously (`Document 02` & `Document 04`), Cogent's `WebSearchRouter.searchLiveWeb()` relied solely on Gemini's built-in `tools: [{ googleSearch: {} }]` grounding with a 12-second timeout and capped at 6 URLs sharing a single truncated 800-character `summaryText`. In real-world Nigerian and African regulatory work, single-engine Gemini search exhibits four severe failure modes:

1. **Missed `.gov.ng` & `.go.ke` Nested PDF Circulars:** African central bank circulars are commonly uploaded with non-semantic filenames (e.g., `BSD-DIR-CON-02-014.pdf` or `/Out/2025/...`) with zero OpenGraph metadata. Standard LLM grounding returns news summaries *about* a circular while missing the primary PDF link itself.
2. **Shallow Context Extraction:** Gemini's `googleSearch` tool exposes only opaque grounding URLs + titles—not full extracted PDF text or multi-paragraph regulatory tables. Writing custom HTML/PDF scrapers for dozens of government CMS platforms fails due to Cloudflare WAFs, ASPX postbacks, and SSL quirks.
3. **Zero Geographic & News Targeting:** Standard grounding does not guarantee Nigeria-localized Google SERPs (`gl=ng`, `location="Lagos, Nigeria"`) or same-day Nigerian financial enforcement coverage (`Nairametrics`, `BusinessDay`, `TechCabal`), causing stale global commentary to outrank fresh Lagos regulatory notices.
4. **No Commercial Quota Precision:** A raw Gemini grounding call bundles search and generation opaquely, preventing strict per-tier monthly metering, clean multi-provider query caching, or regional scoping (`NG`-only vs. `Pan-Africa`).

## 4.2 Why Both `SerpAPI` AND `Exa AI` Together (Complementary Dual-Engine Synergy)

| Capability Axis | **SerpAPI** (`google` + `google_news` Engines) | **Exa AI** (`/search` Neural + `/contents` Extraction) | Combined Synergy with **141-PDF Vault** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Core Strength** | **Exact Keyword, Circular Code & Geo-Localized `.gov.*` PDF Discovery** | **Neural Semantic Understanding + Clean Full-Document & PDF Text Extraction** | **Authoritative Baseline + Real-Time Freshness + Deep Legal Interpretation** |
| **Nigerian Circular Codes** | Excels at exact string matches: `"BSD/DIR/GEN/LAB/14/001"`, `"FPR/DIR/PUB/CIR"`, `"CBN/NDPC/SEC"` | Discovers conceptual equivalents even when titles differ (*"digital assets custody rules"* -> VASP framework) | Cross-references discovered circular IDs against existing Vault instruments to detect amendments/repeals |
| **Government `.gov.ng` & Africa Portals** | Targeted Google dorks: `site:cbn.gov.ng OR site:sec.gov.ng filetype:pdf` localized with `gl=ng`, `hl=en`, `location="Lagos, Nigeria"` (or `gl=ke\|gh\|za\|rw\|eg`) | Curated `includeDomains` filtering across African regulators + Tier-1 legal advisories | Surfaces primary regulator PDFs (`SerpAPI`) AND extracts clean readable provisions (`Exa /contents`) |
| **Breaking News & Enforcement** | `engine=google_news` (`gl=ng`) surfaces same-hour CBN/FCCPC/SEC enforcement fines and directives from `nairametrics.com`, `businessday.ng`, `techcabal.com` | Finds in-depth legal client alerts explaining compliance consequences from `banwo-ighodalo.com`, `aluko-oyebode.com`, `templars-law.com`, `uubo.org`, `olaniwunajayi.net`, `pavestoneslegal.com` | Distinguishes official binding statute (`Vault` / `.gov`) from legal commentary and press intelligence (`Trust Tiers`) |
| **Content Extraction** | Provides Google SERP rank, exact publication date cues, rich snippets, direct `.pdf` download URIs | Single `/contents` API call returns parsed clean markdown/text (`up to 4,000+ chars`) + exact matching `highlights[]` without maintaining fragile scrapers | Eliminates hallucination from short 150-char search snippets by injecting full clean clauses into Gemini context |

# 7. Guardrails, Security & Governance Extensions (`Doc 05` & `Doc 07`)

Extending the existing **7-Layer Guardrail Pipeline** (`guardrails.validator.ts`) and **Zero-Trust Security Architecture** (`Doc 07`) to cover all new workflows and live search engines:

1. **Zero Fabricated Citations or Live URLs:** Any URL referenced in left-pane synthesis or right-pane Work Product memos/matrices **MUST** exist verbatim in the current conversation's verified `live_web_retrievals` allowlist. Any model-generated URL not matching the retrieved allowlist is stripped automatically by the post-generation guardrail pass.
2. **Retrieval Timestamp & Staleness Disclosure:** All `OFFICIAL_REGULATOR_WEB` and `VERIFIED_AFRICAN_LEGAL_MEDIA` citations render explicit retrieval dates (`Retrieved 2026-11-17`) and surface caution badges when a document's effective/publication date precedes a newer Vault amendment.
3. **Indirect Prompt Injection Isolation:** Clean text extracted by `Exa AI /contents` or `SerpAPI` snippets is treated identically to untrusted web payloads—sanitized of markdown control escapes, wrapped inside `<untrusted_live_web_evidence source="..." retrieval_id="...">`, and explicitly bound by system rules never to treat external webpage text as executable instructions.
4. **DDQ Internal-Fact Honesty Enforcement (`WF-3`):** Guardrail scan checks DDQ draft responses against tenant knowledge chunks (`marketing_knowledge_chunks`, `campaign_products`); unsupported affirmative operational assertions trigger `[VERIFY INTERNAL CONTROL]` flags rather than silent fabrication.
5. **Policy Redline Legal Floor Guardrail (`WF-2`):** Policy replacement clauses cannot emit numerical thresholds weaker than active CBN/NFIU/NDPC Vault limits (e.g., KYC limits, retention periods, notification windows).
6. **Strict Composite Tenant Isolation (`Doc 07`):** All new tables (`work_product_artifacts`, `work_product_revisions`, `live_web_search_usage`, and extended `live_web_retrievals`) enforce `tenant_id` composite foreign keys and strict PostgreSQL RLS (`auth.uid()::text IN tenant_memberships`), guaranteeing zero cross-tenant leakage of internal policies, memos, or DDQ answers. Only the shared normalized public-search cache (`live_web_search_cache`) stores strictly public non-tenant query results, keyed solely by query hash + jurisdiction + provider (never storing tenant documents or private queries).
